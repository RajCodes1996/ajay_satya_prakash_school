Campus Information

Ajay Satya Prakash Public School (SPPS) in Jabalpur is a K–12 co-educational day and boarding school located in the Panagar area. Established in 2003 and affiliated with the CBSE board, the eco-friendly campus provides a range of modern learning, residential, and extracurricular facilities.

Highlights

Campus & Infrastructure

Location & Area

Set on a lush, green campus spanning 12 to 15 acres in Panagar, offering a peaceful and eco-friendly environment.

Energy Sustainability

The campus is a pioneer in running entirely on solar energy.

Classrooms & Labs

Features Samsung smart classrooms, fully equipped STEM and IT labs, and advanced science laboratories.

Boarding

Includes clean and inclusive boarding houses designed to foster community, cultural exchange, and a healthy lifestyle.
