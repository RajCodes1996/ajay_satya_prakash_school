About Ajay Satya Prakash Public School

Ajay Satya Prakash Public School (ASPPS) is a CBSE school in Panagar, Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh, educating students from Class 1 to 12. Our campus brings together strong academics, sports, arts, music and activities so that every child grows in knowledge, confidence and character.

Computer learning and practical skill-building that prepare students for a digital future.
